Delete your ORSync account

ORSync, by Our Gym LLC

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open ORSync and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with your password.

Without the app

Email us from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm and delete within 7 days.

Email admin@ourgymapp.com

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email, phone number, and profile photo.
  • Your personal cloud library (cards and settings) if you had one.
  • Your membership in any facility.

What is kept

  • A facility's shared library stays with the facility. Cards, schedules, and notes you added there remain for the rest of the team, with your name on past changes.
  • Cards stored only on your phone stay on your phone until you delete the app.

ORSync never stores patient information.
