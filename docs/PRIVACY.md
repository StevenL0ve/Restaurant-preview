# ORSync Privacy Policy

The current policy is published at
https://stevenl0ve.github.io/Restaurant-preview/privacy.html and its source
is `public/privacy.html` in this repo (deployed with the web app). Edit that
file to change the policy; both stores link to that URL.

Summary: ORSync stores your account (name, email, optional phone and
position) and your library in ORSync Cloud (Google Firebase) when you sign
in, on the device only for guests, and in a facility's shared library once
you join one. No ads, analytics, tracking, or selling of data. Not for
patient information.
