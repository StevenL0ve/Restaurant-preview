The Shop

A neighborhood cellar, not a liquor aisle.

Corktown was built on a simple idea: you shouldn't need a sommelier's vocabulary to find a bottle you'll love. Every label on our shelves earns its place — tasted, vetted, and chosen one at a time, never by a distributor's spreadsheet.

Whether you're grabbing a Tuesday-night red, building a cellar, or hunting the perfect gift, we'll point you to something honest and well-made at a fair price — and tell you exactly why it's good.

Sean Perkins Owner & Curator, Corktown Wine & Spirits

What's on the Shelf

Chosen by hand. Poured with intent.

A tight, rotating selection — depth over clutter. If it's here, there's a reason.

01

Old & New World Wine

Burgundy and Barolo to coastal whites and natural pét-nats — reds, whites, rosé, and orange across every price point.

02

Bourbon & Whiskey

Small-batch and single-barrel bourbon, rye, scotch, and the allocated pours worth chasing — curated for sippers and collectors alike.

03

Agave & Craft Spirits

Additive-free tequila, mezcal, gin, rum, and amari — plus the local distillers and oddball finds you won't see at the big-box store.

04

Champagne & Sparkling

Grower champagne, crémant, cava, and Franciacorta — always a few cold and ready, because some nights don't need a reason.
