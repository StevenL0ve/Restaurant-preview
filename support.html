Common questions

  • How do I join my facility? Settings → Facility, then type the facility name and password your charge desk gave you. An admin at your facility approves the request.
  • Where are my cards stored? On your phone. A facility account adds shared cloud storage for its members; ORSync Pro adds a personal cloud backup synced across your devices.
  • How do I cancel Pro? Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google: Settings → Plans → Manage subscription, or your App Store / Google Play account settings.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete account. It removes your account and its cloud data.
  • Facility pricing? Use the facility pricing button above or in the app; we reply with pricing and an invoice.
