ORSync is a preference-card and case-cart app for surgical teams. It holds no patient information; every free-text field reminds users not to enter any.

Sign in with the demo account. Cards, My day, Surgeons and Settings work right away. Carts, On call, Loaners, Catalog and Sterile processing are a facility's shared OR library: on any of those tabs, tap "Try the demo" to explore them with sample data (no facility needed).

To try card scanning: Cards > Scan / paste > Scan with camera (or Choose photo or PDF), then Create card from this.

The Pro subscriptions are not part of this version; the Plans screen says they are coming soon. They will be submitted with a later version.

Face ID is optional (Settings > Account). Sharing a card uses the system share sheet. The app uses standard HTTPS only.
