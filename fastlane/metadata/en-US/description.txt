Scrub techs and circulating nurses keep a mental rolodex of how every surgeon wants every case set up. ORSync puts it in your pocket, and it belongs to you.

ORGANIZE IT YOUR WAY
• Cards grouped by surgeon and procedure
• Every section a tech needs: positioning, prep, draping, instruments and trays, sutures, supplies, medications, equipment
• Glove size and surgeon quirks front and center
• Favorite the cards you reach for most
• Photos of items, the Mayo stand, and the back table, kept on your device

SET UP THE ROOM WITHOUT MISSING A THING
• Setup mode turns any card into a live pull list with big tap targets
• Check items off as you gather them; a progress bar hits "Case ready" at 100%
• Print a card, a case, or a whole day, each with a QR code back to the live version

YOURS, EVERYWHERE
• Works fully offline (because OR Wi-Fi doesn't)
• Your cards stay on your phone; Pro backs them up to every device
• One search across surgeons, procedures, and every item
• Share a card as a file or text; export your whole library
• Optional Face ID lock

WITH YOUR FACILITY (facility account)
• One shared card library for the whole OR, every change signed and dated
• Case carts for every scheduled case: pull, mark missing, QC, all live
• The on-call board, with everyone's number from their own account
• Loaner tray tracking and the supply catalog with REF photos
• Admins approve who joins and can remove anyone instantly

ORSync is not for patient information. Preference cards describe surgeon and procedure setup, never patients.
